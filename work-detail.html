The Brief

"Because home isn't
where you're from.
It's where you feel it."

The brief was deceptively simple: make people see Airbnb not as a travel service, but as a state of mind. A feeling you can book.

The campaign needed to work across two languages — English and Spanish — without losing its soul in translation. Not adaptation. Transcreation. Which meant understanding not just the words, but the cultural resonance behind each one.

Working as creative director and senior copywriter, I developed the campaign's bilingual voice: warm, specific, and quietly defiant. Every headline had to carry equal weight in both languages — and feel like it was written in that language first.

The Work

A 360 campaign built
to live everywhere
— twice.

The campaign ran across social, digital, and OOH in 12 markets. Each touchpoint required its own adaptation — not just in language, but in tone, reference, and cultural shorthand.

I wrote the hero video script and full VO direction in both English and Spanish, developed the campaign's headline architecture, and created a social series — "Places worth feeling homesick for" — that became the campaign's most-shared content.

The key tension we solved: how to make a global brand feel local in 12 places at once. The answer was specificity — hyperlocal copy that still resonated internationally.
