The Brief

"Food is never just
food. It's memory,
desire, and belonging
— all on a plate."

Netflix needed to launch a new food content vertical for Spanish-speaking audiences across Latin America and Spain. The problem: most "premium food content" was a translation job — English first, Spanish as an afterthought.

La Divina Gula was conceived as something different: a content brand built in Spanish, for Spanish-speaking audiences, rooted in the culture, history, and ritual that surrounds food in the Spanish-speaking world. Not food porn — food with a story.

As creative director and content strategist, I built the editorial universe from scratch: brand voice, content pillars, platform copy, social series, and the long-form features that became campaign assets.

The Work

A content universe
that spoke Spanish
as a first language.

The first decision was strategic: we would not adapt content. We would originate it. Every piece — from the show titles to the Instagram captions — was conceived in Spanish and written for how Spanish-speaking audiences actually talk about food.

I developed the brand's editorial voice (warm, obsessive, deeply knowledgeable but never pretentious), wrote the full content playbook, and created the show descriptions, platform copy, and social series that made the launch feel like a cultural event rather than a product release.

The long-form editorial pieces — food culture essays, chef profiles, recipe origin stories — were designed to live beyond the campaign and become lasting SEO assets for the platform.
