The Project

La Divina Gula is a show about gluttony as a Mexican virtue — food that's excessive, eccentric, and entirely unashamed. The title borrows from Dante on purpose: what the Comedia called a sin, Mexico turned into a way of life. To launch it across LATAM and Spain, we created something the show deserved — a collectible illustrated publication, made in collaboration with dozens of Mexico's finest illustrators, bringing the most outrageous recipes to life the way only Mexican creativity can.
