A B O U T.

Hi, I'm Alex.

I'm a freelance Creative Director and bilingual copywriter based in Mérida, Yucatán — working with teams worldwide in English and Spanish.

Currently, I work within Airbnb's in-house creative team on the Mexican and US Hispanic markets, leading campaigns, seasonal launches, and brand copy that actually sounds like the people it's written for.

Before that, I was part of Netflix's Social Marketing team as a creative consultant — helping launch shows and leading the creative for Nada Que Ver, Netflix's first Spanish-language original podcast.

And before that: 10+ years in advertising agencies, moving from art direction to copywriting to creative direction.

How I ended up working with the world's most interesting teams.

After leading the rebrand of Mexico City's brand identity — one of the largest civic design projects in Latin America — I was burnt out. My wife and I sold everything and went full digital nomad.

Turns out, having a global perspective, a genuinely curious mind, and the ability to adapt to any work style is a pretty rare combination. That led to remote collaborations with 20+ creative teams worldwide — agencies, brand in-house teams, studios across 6 continents — for clients like Square, Uber, and Spotify. Which eventually led to working directly inside those brand teams.

(Working remotely long before Zoom was a household name helped too.)

Life beyond the brief.
Some things that keep me sane.

In 2023, I co-launched Dazzler: Gin for Friends — a gin crafted by friends, for friends, now available on Amazon MX.

Living in Mérida, we've flipped three houses — turning overlooked spaces into something worth sharing. One of them picked up over a million views on YouTube.

During our nomad years: took care of chickens in Scotland, did gardening in London, designed a B&B website in Rome, named a boutique hotel in Mérida, and launched a silk business in Dubai.

I'm always looking for the next interesting challenge — and the right team to do it with.
