Hi, I'm Alex 👋

Currently, I serve as a creative copywriter for Airbnb, focusing on the US Hispanic and Mexican markets.

This role involves working with the talented in-house creative team to develop brand campaigns, seasonal launches, and even product names that resonate with US Hispanic and Mexican communities.

Before this, I was part of Netflix's Social Marketing team as a creative consultant for the special projects team, where I helped launch and promote shows that have likely caught your eye. I also led creative efforts for Nada Que Ver, a Netflix original podcast.

And even before that, I spent over 10 years in advertising agencies, taking on a variety of roles from art direction to copywriting, and eventually moving into creative direction. Most of the work along the way has come through word of mouth. People I've worked with tend to come back.




How did I go from advertising agencies to collaborating with some of the world's most innovative companies?

Well, after a very interesting but demanding project (where we led the revamp of Mexico City's brand identity) I was burned out. So, my wife and I decided to start from scratch, sell all of our possessions, and embrace a fully digital-nomad lifestyle while traveling around. — Turns out, having a global perspective, a very curious mind, and the ability to quickly adapt to any work style makes for a great set of skills.😅

This led me to work remotely with more than 20 creative teams worldwide on various projects for clients such as Square, Uber, and Spotify, which ultimately led to work within the actual brand teams.

(Plus, having worked remotely long before Zoom was more than a camera feature really helped in the process.)


Life beyond the ad world.
Some fun facts, personal projects and more.

In 2023, I took a leap into entrepreneurship by launching my very own gin brand, "Dazzler: Gin for Friends". Crafted by friends for friends, our gin offers a unique blend of freshness and flavor that's perfect for any aspiring mixologist. Available on Amazon 😉

While living in Mérida, Yucatán, we flipped three houses — transforming them from plain spaces into inspiring gems. One of these projects garnered over 1 million views on YouTube.


In short, I'm always on the lookout for new challenges and exciting opportunities to collaborate with forward-thinking people and their amazing teams.
